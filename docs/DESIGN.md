# Design record

The player aid grew from “I'm too lazy to decide. Universe, pick for me.” It reduces decision friction when switching activities. It does not decide important life matters or choose every task. TickTick is one source of activities, not the definition of the game.

## Accepted workflow

1. Gather and cast: hold/release, tap, or activate the button with a keyboard. Cryptographic randomness supplies a fair d20; gestures add a ritual without changing probabilities.
2. Discover: the chosen category expands. Start your own activity or a matching TickTick card immediately. Veto is available here, before the secondary roll. Two thirds of the tablet/desktop space belongs to this choice, one third to optional approaches.
3. Optional approach: a d6 chooses Clear, Build, Begin, Wander, Improve or Delight. It expands to an invitation with personal examples. Six usual categories show the choices; optional categories disclose them on request; Rest and Go Outside skip them. Natural one is a free category choice.
4. Try: the invitation fills the available stage. An own activity retains the approach. A chosen TickTick card drops the approach and shows its own details beside the main category description. A calm pool fills over fifteen minutes. Done records completion sooner; passing the deadline does the same once. Continuing the actual activity is welcome.

The d20 uses the original mapping: Manual Override 1; Keep House 2/18; Tech Support 3; Rest 4; Connect 5; Create 6/14; Manage Money 7; Organize 8/16; Cook 9; Move 10/19; Read 11; Browse 12; Watch 13; Work 15; Reflect 17; Go Outside 20. Repeated results are permitted; no hidden anti-repeat weighting.

## Credits and autonomy

Provisional values, easy to adjust after use: one credit for a handled session, one extra for starting a TickTick card, one extra for every three consecutive accepted results. One free category veto per local calendar day; later vetoes cost three credits and reset progress toward the third-result bonus. An unavailable/impossible/inappropriate result can reroll free without affecting credits or bonus progress. Credits carry over, and there is no daily streak penalty. Leaving early earns nothing; leaving after the deadline first records the elapsed session.

Rules of Good Sense remain visible through disclosure, with free reroll during the invitation and leave actions. Major decisions, health, safety and obligations stay under human control. Satisfying momentum takes priority over further casts.

## TickTick matching

Explicit category tags match the user's existing organization. Up to three open, available cards; today first, then oldest due date, then undated. Date ranges including today count as today. Timed dates use their timezone; floating dates retain the supplied calendar day. Future starts are withheld. Selecting a card reads its details again and snapshots them for offline Try/history. Failed refreshes can use saved details; completed cards are removed on selection. The app never completes or modifies tasks.

## Choices deliberately deferred

Cross-device synchronization, account backend, automatic completion of TickTick cards, notifications, and broader suggestion sources are outside this first app. More personal examples for other categories and adjustments to modes/credit amounts should follow actual use. Physical iPad Safari installation remains to be verified on the user's device.
